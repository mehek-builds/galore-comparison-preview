# FAQ comparison notes

These questions are Galore copy, not copied from the four sites. The research pattern is to answer practical objections plainly. Seven topics recur on at least two reference sites; the creator/investor question is mostly Galore-specific.

| Galore question | Reference overlap | Why it belongs here |
|---|---|---|
| What happens when I enter a belief? | Astor Thesis; Conviction idea-to-strategy; Volaren idea-to-trade | Explain the path from an idea to possible investments and a decision. |
| Can I use Galore today? | Conviction separates paper and live; Volaren answers practice access and its waitlist; Astor separates its account features | State the current paper-only, invited access. |
| Can I test an idea before trading? | Conviction emphasizes backtesting; Volaren compares simulated strategies with the S&P 500; Astor shows thesis performance | Explain history, benchmark and limits. |
| Which investments does Galore explore? | Conviction, Volaren and Mochatrade list asset types | Scope Galore to stocks, ETFs and crypto. |
| Who can I follow? | Volaren shows investors and public filings; Galore's creator and politician directory is its own product feature | Keep this because Galore visitors ask it, without implying endorsement. |
| Will Galore place a trade without asking? | Astor and Volaren answer approval; Conviction separates paper and live; Mochatrade shows order status | State the current limit and control clearly. |
| How much does it cost? | Astor and Volaren give prices; Conviction asks whether a user pays; Mochatrade lists fees | Surface the existing Galore pricing page. |
| Where does the evidence come from? | Astor describes linked research; Conviction names financial data and filings; Volaren answers information provenance | Explain what a visitor can check and disclose that this preview does not fetch sources. |

References: https://www.astor.app/ , https://www.convictiontrade.ai/ , https://www.volaren.ai/ , https://mochatrade.com/ .

The isolated draft is a design preview. It does not enroll users, verify a new research request, connect a brokerage, or place an order.
