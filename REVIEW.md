# Galore design walkthrough review

The prototype adapts applicable presentation patterns from the four-site comparison in `COVERAGE.md`. That file now distinguishes what is shown, what has been adapted, and what is held because it would imply unsupported proof, capabilities, or conflicting brand choices. It is a standalone preview; the real Galore website and dashboard remain separate.

The public site keeps the waitlist as its primary action, exposes a concrete belief in the hero, and links to an interactive dashboard. A timed visual walkthrough immediately after the hero types that electricity belief, connects three possible investments, draws the evidence and risk sequence, presents a practice order for explicit approval, and shows a fictional watch update. Visitors can pause, replay or select a scene. The six feature modules and FAQ retain secondary details. The site uses clear paper/live distinctions, direct About and Pricing links, an illustrative ticker strip, and a preview-only form. Unverified named trade badges and fictional news attribution were removed.

The dashboard preview has five primary destinations. Home opens a sample research flow; Explore shows three ideas; the research view explains possible connections, evidence, risks, and a fictional comparison layout with period controls; Watchlist saves example beliefs; Portfolio allows a reviewed simulated order against $10,000 of paper cash; People separates creators, politicians and investors; More explains account access, brokerage status, AI connections, rules, and deletion. A weekly leaderboard shows an honest empty state. All sample selections are held in session storage and can be cleared. Nothing sends an email, writes to the real waitlist, fetches live sources, or executes a trade.
