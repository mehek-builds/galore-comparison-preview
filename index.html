FAQs

What does Galore show me?

Start with an idea, see investments connected to it, read the evidence and risks, and compare historical performance. You can also follow public trades and build a paper portfolio.

Can I trade on Galore today?

You can use a paper account after signing up. Live brokerage trading is not available yet. Join the waitlist.

Who can I follow on Galore?

Explore public trades from creators like Jeremy Lefebvre and Jim Cramer, and disclosed holdings from people like Nancy Pelosi, Warren Buffett and Cathie Wood. Want someone else? Request a creator.

Can I test an investing idea before I trade it?

The interactive preview shows example connections, evidence questions, and risks. A real backtest should include dated prices, weights, fees, assumptions, and a same-period benchmark. No performance is claimed by this preview. Explore the sample research view.

Where can I track my positions?

The paper portfolio walkthrough shows simulated cash and practice orders. Actual account data would appear in the app after access.

Does joining the waitlist create an account?

No. The waitlist is a request for access. An invitation would explain email confirmation and paper-account setup. Access is offered in batches, with no guaranteed timing.

How much does Galore cost?

Waitlist signup is free. Membership pricing and any trading fees will be shared before paid access is offered; this preview does not quote a fee.
