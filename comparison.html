What you should actually notice

Hero and opening

The preview uses “Start with an idea. See where it leads,” an electricity example, and a dashboard preview link. The live homepage still opens with “Invest in what you believe.”

Product walkthrough

A new timed sequence types the belief, branches to investments, reveals evidence and a risk, then reaches paper approval and a saved update.

Creator source trail

The old small feature cards have been replaced with a wide, animated screen showing the original post, an update, and the public record.

Selectable research

VRT, CEG, and an ETF example change the reason, source question, and risk in the research screen.

Paper plan

Move the amount slider to see an illustrative allocation and remaining paper cash. Review prepares a simulated order; it cannot trade.

Separate dashboard walkthrough

The preview links to a sample dashboard with research, watchlist, people, portfolio, and account states. It is separate from the real Galore app.
